**Goal.** After this slice a plain `tensor`, a plain `linear`, and a name with no declaration are
binary32 by default; `f64` is the explicit spelling for binary64. Slice 1 (merge `574a633`) already
built the test-side lever (`leanncd/test/Eval/ExplicitF64.lean`); this slice flips the default and
migrates the 11 test modules the flip breaks.

test modules (277 measured failing assertions across SIX modules, 8+71+101+48+48+1; `StructuralTest` and
`ComplexElementTypeTest` have no measured count, being fixed in the prototype and verified by a
targeted build; the three downstream modules are unmeasured), and three modules are
unprototyped. That is more than ~6 work items and a classification step that can surface a
production issue, so the Direct path does not fit.
