### F1 [Minor] `privateSlots` is unauthenticated; the exact-sequence check no longer forbids dropping a real output
`Prepared.lean` `checkPreparedBindings`. Scenario: take `repeatPredPrepared` (slots: `Y#1`=a, `Y#2`=b, `Y`=c, `Z`=d), set `privateSlots := #[a,b,c]` and `materializedNames := #[Z]`. Every hidden slot is published, `expected = [d]`, `actual = [d]`: accepted, and `unpack` silently omits `Y`, `materializedSignatures` omits `Y`. The old exact-sequence check rejected this (`publicationSlots`); no fixture pins the new behaviour either way. Names were already unauthenticated (spike S3), but the old check was the one guarantee that the visible list could not omit a real output. Acceptable for slice 1: `prepareEvalPlan` is the only producer, `PreparedPlan` constructors are only hand-built in tests/spikes, and the plan records `privateSlots` as unauthenticated (p2a Q1). Minimal hardening (not applied): store hidden `SlotBinding`s (name + slot) instead of bare slots and require each hidden name to have the shape `<base>#<digits>` with `<base>` present among the visible names and its slot LATER than the hidden slot (the collector is emitted after its writers). That makes hiding `Y` or `Z` impossible without also forging a visible base. Cheapest alternative: add one fixture documenting the accepted forgery so the weakening is visible, and a one-line caveat in `PlanBindings`' docstring.
