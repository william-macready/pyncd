## Major

**M1. Line 42, dictionary row "Laws of `sequence`, Proposition 3.9": "none" and "Production
leaves the laws to prose" are false.**
Quoted: `| none | Mini proves strictness as an iff. Production leaves the laws to prose. |`
`LeanNCD/Semantics/Completeness.lean` proves `sequence_sound`, `sequence_complete` and
`sequence_some_iff` (line 45: `sequence f = some v ↔ ∀ i, f i = some (v i)`). The same file has
`tab_complete`, `reduce_empty`, `reduce_defined_iff`, `reduce_sum`, `at_some_iff`,
`prim_some_iff` and `ready_complete_extension`. Chapter 3 cites several of these
(03-partiality.md lines 478, 574, 599, 901).
Fix: set the production cell to `sequence_some_iff` (with `sequence_sound`, `sequence_complete`)
and the "what differs" cell to "Same iff; production states it for the dependent `sequence`, in
`Completeness.lean`." Also add `reduce_empty`, `reduce_defined_iff` to the Reduction row
(line 43) and `ready_complete_extension` to the ready-evaluation row (line 52). The
`Completeness` module is also missing from the Section 5(a) import list and from every table.

**M2. Line 516, "Add `tab` and `at`": the chapter contradicts Chapter 3 about layout
independence.**
Quoted: "Chapter 3 notes that the layout independence of `tab` is an argument in prose, not a Lean
theorem, so the agent should not assume it is already proved."
Chapter 3 (03-partiality.md lines 595-601) says the opposite. It says layout independence "follows
from `tab_complete` in `Completeness.lean`", whose statement does not mention the layout, and
the theorem exists (`Completeness.lean` line 50, `evalWith ops p (.tab sh layout body) γ = some v ↔ ∀ c, evalWith ops p body (γ, c) = some (v c)`).
Fix: replace the sentence by "Chapter 3 derives layout independence from the Lean theorem
`tab_complete`; an agent changing `tab` must keep that theorem (and `evalWith_stable`'s `tab`
case) compiling."

**M3. Line 516, same paragraph: "this needs the dependent `sequence`, not `sequenceFin`" is false.**
In production the `tab` clause calls `sequence (fun i => evalWith ... (γ, layout.enumerate i))`
with a constant family `A`. A non-dependent `Fin n → Option α` to `Option (Fin n → α)`
suffices, and `sequenceFin` has exactly that type. The dependent form is needed only for
`prim`, where each argument has its own type from `Registry.input`.
Fix: "`tab` sequences over `Fin layout.count` with the same `sequenceFin`; the dependent
`sequence` is needed only for `prim`, whose arguments have different types."

**M4. Line 474, Intent Layer / Lean analogy: "`Declarations.unique : Function.Injective uid` is
the same invariant" is not the same invariant, and the field belongs to `Declarations`, not to a
program.**
Quoted: "`Axis` carries a `uid`, and `Declarations.unique : Function.Injective uid` is the same
invariant as a field a program must prove before it exists."
`Declarations.unique` (Types.lean line 63) says tensor UIDs are injective. `Axis.uid` is never
used outside `Types.lean`: axis equality is `deriving DecidableEq` on the pair `(uid, extent)`,
and no theorem says an axis is identified by its UID alone. The CLAUDE.md invariant is about
axes, so this is the wrong counterpart. The chapter's own honesty rule (CONVENTIONS section 5,
"analogy") applies.
Fix: "The Lean counterpart is partial and about tensors rather than axes: `Declarations.unique :
Function.Injective uid` forces tensor identity to be by UID, and `Axis` carries a `uid` field
but no lemma says axis identity is by UID alone. Treat this as an analogy."

**M5. Line 405, wrong citation of the proposal's Section 10.1.**
Quoted: "The status column of the proposal (Section 10.1, steps 5 and 6) is the honest list of what
is proved and what is a boundary."
In `categorical_tensor_logic_semantics.md` Section 10.1 the column is named "Current boundary",
not "status". Steps 5 and 6 are model gluing and witness spans, not the ranked results the
paragraph is about; ranked phases are step 9. Fix: "The 'Current boundary' column of the
proposal's Section 10.1 (step 9 for ranked phases) is the honest list ...".

## Minor

**m3. Line 399, "`collect_grouped` and `collect_relabel` are the bridge to the single global

Lean has no global pushforward across tensors: `Program.collect` is per tensor. The theorems
relate per-statement grouping and relabeling inside one target, which is what Chapter 4 says

## Verdict

The chapter is accurate where it matters most for the user's honesty requirements. The mini
file's proved results (`run_sound`, `run_sound_failed`, `measure_decreases`, the transpose and
span laws, `scorer_models₁`, `scorer_no_model₀`) are described correctly, and so are the checked
but unproved ones (fuel sufficiency, chain termination, `AdmInput` for η₁). The compile output
is quoted exactly, and Examples 6.1 and 6.2 and exercises 1, 3 and 6 reproduce as stated. The
defects are in the production side of the dictionary: one false "none" with a false "prose only"
claim (M1), a statement about Chapter 3 that Chapter 3 contradicts (M2), a false claim that `tab`
needs the dependent `sequence` (M3), a wrong Lean counterpart for the CLAUDE.md axis-identity
invariant (M4), a mis-cited section of the proposal (M5), and non-verbatim quotations (M6). None
changes what the mini file proves, but M1 and M2 would lead an agent to believe that production
has no strictness or layout theorems when it does. After the six Major fixes and the Minor
wording changes (especially m1, m6, m10, m12) the chapter is sound.
