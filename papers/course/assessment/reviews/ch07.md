## Critical

**C1. Line 51: an invented disagreement between sources about `checkPlan_iff`.**
Quoted: "Third, the table says the exact checker theorems `checkPlan_iff` and `checkPlanF_iff` "remain". Part V Section 31.1 says `checkPlan` decides `Valid` exactly through `checkPlan_iff`. The two sources disagree, and this chapter does not settle which is current."
Wrong: the table sentence (`tensor_logic_semantics.md` line 242) is "`Valid.layout` supplies all-boundary alias safety; exact `checkPlan_iff` and `checkPlanF_iff` remain." "Remain" there means "remain exact/true" after slice 4 (compare `plan_layer_slice4_plan.md` line 49: "`checkPlan_iff` and `checkPlanF_iff` remain exact"). Both theorems exist in Lean (`LeanNCD/Semantics/Plan/Validity.lean:460`, `Plan/FusionCorrectness.lean:36`). There is no disagreement, so the chapter tells students a status-table row is open when it is not. The quoted single word is lifted out of its sentence.
Fix: delete the third caution and change "Three cautions apply" to "Two cautions apply". If the sentence is kept in any form, quote the whole clause and say the table lists both theorems as still holding, consistent with Part V Section 31.1.

## Major

**M1. Line 116: "In Turi and Plotkin's setting, that compatibility law takes the form of a distributive law" is not supported by the cited Turi-Plotkin document.**
`categorical_operational_semantics.md` never uses "distributive" (grep: zero hits). Section 1.3 says only "Suitable natural transformations describe the third [ingredient]" and "A further compatibility law connects these structures" (lines 106-118); Appendix A.8 defers the law to "the next topic" (line 1070). The only place in the sources that pairs the words is the proposal, Section 8 (lines 1865-1866, "Turi-Plotkin distributive law") and Section 10.3, and there it is a name for the missing artifact, not a statement that the TP document identifies the compatibility law as a distributive law. The sentence sits directly after a citation to the TP document, so a reader will attribute it there.
Fix: replace with "The proposal calls the missing artifact a Turi-Plotkin distributive law ([proposal, Section 8](...)); the Turi-Plotkin document describes it only as a compatibility law between the algebra and coalgebra structures." Or delete the sentence.

**M2. Line 90: two different numerical profiles are conflated, and the quoted status is attached to the wrong one.**
Quoted: "The Lean numerical profile uses exact rationals, and the status table says it "is not a floating-point semantics."" In the source (`tensor_logic_semantics.md` lines 268-270) the phrase belongs to the bounded source layer's numerical profile: "claims exact bit agreement only for programs with f64-declared integral values of magnitude at most $2^{20}$ and one definition per left-hand side; it is not a floating-point semantics." The exact-rational profile is a different sentence (lines 249-251: "The verified runtime profile uses exact rationals"). Also the phrase is in the prose after the table, not in a table row.
Fix: split the two. "The verified runtime profile uses exact rationals. Separately, the bounded source layer's numerical profile claims bit agreement only for f64-declared integral values of magnitude at most $2^{20}$ with one definition per left-hand side, and 'is not a floating-point semantics' (status text after the table)."

**M3. Line 51: "every compiled-plan row holds only for the slice 1 to 4 profile" contradicts the chapter's own table.**
The row for "Section 33.1 instance" (line 68) is slice 5 (`Plan.ScanHistory`; source line 244 "Proved (slice 5...)"). The Lemma 32.1 row is not stated for the slice 1 to 4 profile at all; it is qualified as "failure half for the transactional singleton kernel only".
Fix: "Every Lemma 31.2 and Theorem 31.3 row holds only for the slice 1 to 4 profile; the Section 33.1 row is a separate bounded slice 5 instance; the Lemma 32.1 row has its own failure-half qualification." Also note that the status text dates itself to a 2026-10-10 snapshot while describing slice 4 as merged through 2026-10-12 and slice 5 (source lines 191-208). The chapter repeats the 10-10 date without noting the source is internally inconsistent.

**M4. Line 72: the Summary does not describe "four experimental layers", and the third layer's quote is taken from a different paragraph.**
(a) The Summary (lines 12-174) enumerates a "five layers" approach (index geometry through implementation refinement, lines 32-42) and then reports at least eight proved results: static composition, model composition, categorical witness semantics, ranked operational composition, associative operational composition, ranked operational interpretation, operational-to-witness comparison, plus Section 6.15 (affine reads, line 1672) listed in the TOC. "Four experimental layers" is the chapter's own selection, silently omitting model gluing, ranked phases (Section 6.11), and operational associativity (Section 6.12), and it is credited to the Summary. The word "experimental" appears in Section 10.2 (line 2285, "experimental profile"), not in the Summary.
(b) The quote "an operational bridge toward a process interpretation, not a proof of process-category laws" is the closing sentence of the paragraph "Ranked operational composition" (Summary lines 91-103). The chapter attaches it to the third layer, which it describes as "a source category, a full-observation target category, and a Mathlib functor". That description is the paragraph "Ranked operational interpretation" (lines 115-128), which carries no such sentence.
Fix: say "The Summary reports several proved results; four are most relevant here" and name them, add the ranked operational composition (phases) as its own item and attach the quote to it; or restructure the list to follow the Summary paragraphs one to one. Drop "experimental" or cite Section 10.2.

**M5. Line 88: "General cyclic solvers. These are not part of the semantics" misstates the specification.**
Part III Section 20.4 and 20.2 say the model relation does not require acyclic dependencies and is a fixed-point characterization; cyclic programs have a denotation (their model set). What Part IV Section 28 excludes is a solver: "General cyclic solvers remain a separately justified extension, not an implicit fallback." The chapter's own quote two sentences later is correct, but its lead sentence is stronger. Part 5 (line 109-111) likewise calls solving a "separate extension".
Fix: "A solver for general cyclic systems is not part of the reference machine. The model relation still applies to cyclic programs."
Related, Minor in itself: "The categorical proposal gives the reason" (same paragraph) overreaches. Section 9.3 explains why the unique-model criterion is not compositional for cyclic systems, not why the specification omits a solver. Fix: "The categorical proposal gives a related warning."

**M6. Line 118: hedges in the Turi-Plotkin document are dropped, so the benefits read as unconditional.**
- 2.2 says "For appropriate specifications, the categorical framework proves that this equivalence [strong bisimilarity] is a congruence." The chapter says "behavioural equivalence respects the constructors" with no condition and without naming strong bisimilarity.
- 2.3 begins "Under the relevant hypotheses, the framework constructs a domain of behaviours..." The chapter says the behaviours of components determine the behaviour of the whole, without the hypotheses.
- 2.1 says "This does not eliminate proof obligations. It moves them to a more reusable level: show that the specification fits the framework." The chapter says "a new constructor need not be proved from scratch" and omits the obligation to show the rules fit.
Fix: add "for specifications that meet the framework's hypotheses" to all three, name strong bisimilarity in the first, and add the 2.1 sentence about showing the specification fits the framework.

## Verdict

The chapter is a faithful summary in most places: citations resolve, quotes are verbatim, Lean names are copied exactly, CONVENTIONS section 4 is respected, and section 4 restates the DESIGN decisions in the right order. It should not ship as is. One Critical defect (C1) tells students that a Lean status-table row is open when the theorem exists and the source says it "remain[s] exact". The Major defects are a Turi-Plotkin claim (distributive law) that the cited document does not make, a conflation of two numerical profiles, a slice-profile statement that contradicts the chapter's own table, a Summary attribution that merges or misplaces four layers and one quote, an overstatement about cyclic solvers, and dropped hedges in the Turi-Plotkin benefits. All are local edits. After the Critical and Major items are fixed, the remaining Minor items are polish.

Counts: Critical 1, Major 6, Minor 18.
