## Major

**M1. Line 308-310: the definedness order is described as an order on results with $\bot$ at the bottom (fact 3).**
Quoted: "A word on fact 3: pointwise, $\preceq$ amounts to comparing results with $\bot$ placed below every value."
What is wrong: CONVENTIONS fact 3 and spec 18.1 ("No order or least-fixed-point interpretation is attached to it") say $\bot$ carries no order. This sentence explicitly installs the flat order on $\mathrm{Result}(\tau)$, which is exactly the domain-theoretic reading the chapter must avoid. The two sentences that follow restrict how it is *used*, but the sentence itself attaches an order to $\bot$, and it contradicts Section 7 line 886 ("No order on $\bot$ as a semantic value"). The source (categorical notes 3.3) defines $\preceq$ only as a relation between partial functions.
Fix: replace the sentence with: "A word on fact 3: $\preceq$ is a relation between two partial maps with the same source and target, namely inclusion of their graphs. It is not an order on $\mathrm{Result}(\tau)$, and $\bot$ remains an unordered tag. We use it only to compare two computations, typically a program and a proposed rewrite of it." Keep the final sentence about least elements and chains.

**M2. Line 409: the central display of Section 4 does not render.**
Quoted: `$$[\![E]\!]_\rho:D\rightharpoonupV_\tau,\qquad ...$$`
What is wrong: `\rightharpoonupV_\tau` is parsed as the undefined control sequence `\rightharpoonupV`; MathJax/KaTeX show an error in place of the definition of $[\![E]\!]_\rho$ as a partial map, which every later row of Definition 3.15 depends on.
Fix: `D\rightharpoonup V_\tau`.

**M3. Lines 547-548, 572-573, 864-866, 896-898: the chapter misstates what Lean proves about Section 18; `Completeness.lean` is ignored.**
Quoted: "(this is our observation, not a Lean theorem)" (548); "(again our argument, not a Lean theorem)" (572-573); "the only Lean theorems are those quoted in Section 6" (897-898).
What is wrong: `LeanNCD/Semantics/Completeness.lean` proves, for exactly the clauses Definition 3.15 organizes: `sequence_some_iff` (Definition 3.8 for `Fin n`), `tab_complete` (`evalWith ops p (.tab sh layout body) γ = some v ↔ ∀ c, evalWith ops p body (γ, c) = some (v c)`), `foldValues_defined_iff`, `reduce_defined_iff`, `reduce_empty` (empty reduction is `ops.zero` independently of its body), `foldValues_some_map_sum` / `reduce_sum` (value of a successful reduction is the sum under `AddCommMonoid`), `at_some_iff`, `registry_apply_some_iff`, `prim_some_iff`, and `ready_complete_extension` (existence of the complete extension used in Definition 3.24). `expression_readiness_plan.md` maps these to spec 18.3-18.4 and 23.3. Consequences: (a) layout independence of the `.tab` clause is an immediate corollary of `tab_complete`, whose right side does not mention `layout`, so "not a Lean theorem" is misleading; (b) the definedness half and the value half of "foldValues = sequence then fold" are `foldValues_defined_iff` and `foldValues_some_map_sum`; (c) the existence half of Definition 3.24's well-definedness is `ready_complete_extension`, not `ready_consistent` alone; (d) the Section 7 sentence is false as written.
Fix: (a) at 572-573 write "This follows from `tab_complete` in `Completeness.lean`, whose statement does not mention the layout." (b) at 547-548 write "Its definedness and value are characterized by `foldValues_defined_iff` and `foldValues_some_map_sum` (`Completeness.lean`); the fused statement itself is Exercise 7." (c) at 864 add "the existence of an extension is `ready_complete_extension`." (d) at 897-898 replace with "the Lean theorems used are those quoted in Sections 4 and 6, from `Readiness.lean` and `Completeness.lean`." Optionally add `reduce_empty` beside the "an empty reduction evaluates no body" bullet (lines 451-457), since it is the Lean form of that clause.

## Verdict

The mathematics is sound. Kleisli(Option) = Pfn, the non-cartesian monoidal
structure, `sequence` as a distributive law, the definedness order, and
Proposition 3.13 are all correct. The proposition's direction, both equality
conditions and the strict counterexample agree with fact 11 and with
categorical notes 3.3. The Kleisli reading reproduces spec section 18 clause by
clause, including strictness, the empty reduction that evaluates no body,
strict tabulation, selection that does not bypass an undefined component, and
primitive domains. The footprint table and read stability match spec 23.2-23.3.
Ready evaluation keeps waiting distinct from $\bot$. Running and spec numbers
are exact, and every Lean snippet and Section 6 theorem paraphrase is faithful.
No finding is Critical. Three Major items should be fixed before release:
- the sentence that gives $\bot$ an order (fact 3);
- a display that fails to render;
- Lean statements that overlook `Completeness.lean` and so wrongly say that
  layout independence and the fold characterization are not in Lean, and that
  no other Lean theorems exist.

The Minor items are notation drift from CONVENTIONS and Chapter 0, a few
overclaims about "theorem, not policy", and small precision gaps.

Counts: Critical 0, Major 3, Minor 17.
